Storage and backups

Current version stores data locally in this browser on this device. No cloud sync or AI backend is configured. Clear browser data or uninstall without a backup and you may lose it.

Privacy: do not enter confidential BCI project or employee data in this personal prototype. Add authenticated, encrypted company storage before business use.

Voice and notifications

Voice dictation uses the browser's speech-recognition capability where supported. Some browsers send audio to their speech provider, so do not dictate sensitive information. For reliable private offline transcription, a future native Android version will need an on-device speech model.
