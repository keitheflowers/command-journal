Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Plain-language summary: your data stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on a feature that needs it. Each one is listed below.

What the app stores

Tasks, calendar items, money (transactions, budgets, accounts, bills, savings goals), fitness, meals, fasting and workout data you enter. It is stored only in your browser’s storage on this device, plus daily restore points in the same browser. We (the app’s makers) do not receive, see or store this data.

What leaves your phone, and only if you use it

  • Voice commands: your phone’s browser (for example Chrome) turns speech into text using its own speech service, which may process audio on Google’s servers under Google’s privacy policy. Don’t say account numbers or other sensitive details out loud.
  • Vector AI (off by default): when you turn it on with your own Anthropic API key, the words of your request, plus summaries of your open tasks, bills, budget, accounts, savings goals, grocery list and family fitness/meal data needed to answer, are sent to Anthropic to generate a response, under Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy. Your key is stored only on this device and is never included in backups.
  • Food search: searching USDA foods sends your search words to the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s FoodData Central API.
  • Backups you share: when you tap “Back up now”, a backup file goes wherever you choose (Google Drive, email, and so on). That copy is then governed by that service.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No analytics or crash reporting is built in.
  • No account is required.

Security

You can lock the app with a PIN (stored only as a salted hash) and your phone’s fingerprint or face unlock. The lock keeps people out of the app. It does not encrypt the browser storage underneath, so keep your phone itself locked too.

Your choices

  • Export everything any time (Settings → Backups → Download file).
  • Delete everything by clearing this site’s data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app. This cannot be undone without a backup.
  • Turn Vector AI off any time (Settings → Vector AI), and use “Forget key” to remove your key.

Children

The app is meant to be set up and managed by adults. It is not directed at children under 13.
